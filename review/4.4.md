**Definitions stated in full.** The cell value is
`carry / duration × 1e4` — basis points of carry per year, per year of
modified duration. Rows are the 48 `country-tenor` buckets, grouped by
country in `CHART4_COUNTRY_ORDER` (`US, GB, DE, FR, JP, CA`) and ascending in
tenor inside each country, with a black rule between countries. Columns are
every month in `carry.parquet`, so a bucket that does not exist in a month is
NaN and is drawn **grey**, never white and never zero. The colormap is
`RdBu_r`, diverging and centred on zero: red is positive carry, blue
negative. The scale is symmetric and clipped at the 99th percentile of
|value|, which is **±163 bp**.

**One choice the plan does not make.** The plan says "diverging colormap
centred at 0" and does not say where the scale ends. Unclipped, the US
1980s reaches −846 bp and every post-2000 month washes out to white. The
clip is at the 99th percentile of |carry/duration| over all 23,680
bucket-months, it is symmetric so zero stays at the centre of the colormap,
and the limit is printed in the figure title. 237 of 23,680 cells (1.0%)
saturate.

## Not verified

- **That the 99th percentile is the right clip.** It is a choice, stated in
  the title of the figure and in the docstring, and the numbers behind it are
  in the third finding. Any other percentile would be as defensible; nothing
  downstream reads the scale.
- **That the per-country small multiples are legible at print size.** They
  are 11 × 3.2 inches at 130 dpi with 6-point row labels, which is fine on
  screen. The report of 6.4 is where that gets settled.

## Open

- Nothing. The chart draws what `carry.parquet` contains; every feature that
  looked odd on it — the JP 1970s saturation, the US 1980s extremes, the grey
  blocks — traces to a number in `carry_missing.csv` or
  `universe_by_month.csv`.
