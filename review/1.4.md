## Not verified
- Whether MOF's "benchmark" yields before 1986 at 1–9 years are true par yields of on-the-run JGBs or compound yields as quoted in the Japanese market convention at the time (simple yield vs compound). The file says "Interest Rate" and nothing more. If the pre-1986 Japanese quotation were simple yield, the bootstrap in 1.9 would be applied to a mis-typed quote for those years; the JP 10-year only exists from 1986-07, so those years cannot enter the strategy sample anyway. Settled by reading MOF's methodology note before Session 1 part B if the gate needs pre-1986 Japan.
