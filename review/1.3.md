## Not verified
- The 0.25 and 0.5-year reconstructions are the Svensson function evaluated below the Bundesbank's own shortest published residual maturity; they are model values, not observations, like every other DE tenor. Whether the Bundesbank's fit is anchored by bills at that end is not checked here.
- September 2026 is a partial month (last parameter print 2026-09-17), stamped 2026-09-30; `strategy_end` excludes it.
