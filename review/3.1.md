**Rules stated in full.**

*The tenor set (amendment 1, as answered in issue #15).* A standard tenor is
in a country's PCA set if it is present in at least
`config.pca.tenor_presence_min` (0.95) of that country's months counted from
**the first month the country has a 10-year zero**. The pooled set is the
intersection of the six. The stop condition is kept and does not fire:
5 tenors for a country or 6 pooled raises `TenorSetTooSmall`.

*The input (amendment 3).* `data/processed/curves_zero.parquet`, standard
tenors, in `(y_t − y_{t−1}) × 1e4` basis points. No Phase 2 fitted yield is
read. A change row exists only where month `t` **and the previous calendar
month end** are both complete on the set.

*The signs (amendment 2).* PC1 loading at 10y > 0; PC2 at (longest − 2)y > 0;
PC3 at the middle tenor of the set > 0 (the lower of the two middles when the
set is even); PC4+ first non-zero element positive.

*The two pooled fits (issue #15 answer 5).* `scope = "pooled"` on the
intersection `1,2,3,5,7,10` is the primary fit and **the only pooled fit any
step after 3.3 reads**. `scope = "pooled_20y"` on `1,2,3,5,7,10,20`, over the
months every country has complete, is reported only.

## Not verified

- **That the pooled demeaning is the plan's.** Each country's block is
  demeaned by its own column means before stacking, so the stacked matrix has
  an exactly zero column mean and the `pca` demeaning is a no-op on it. The
  arithmetic is asserted only implicitly, by
  `test_scores_reconstruct_changes` on a single block. A test that stacks two
  blocks with very different means and checks the loadings against a
  hand-demeaned reference would settle it.
- **That the 95% threshold is the right one.** It is the owner's number. The
  sets are not near the line except JP's 30-year (51.9%) and GB's 20-year
  (88.4%), both comfortably out; nothing sits between 0.93 and 0.97, so the
  sets are not sensitive to the exact threshold. A sweep of the threshold
  from 0.90 to 0.99 printing the sets would settle it.
- **That `pooled_20y` is unread downstream.** It is stated in PLAN.md 3.1 and
  in the module docstring, and no step after 3.3 exists yet. A grep test in
  6.4 asserting that no module outside `pca.py` and `charts.py` mentions
  `pooled_20y` would settle it.
