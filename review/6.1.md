### Definitions stated in full

Per held bucket `j` at month `t`, `w_j` the capital weight, `c_j` the par
yield the bond was bought at (`returns.coupon`), `rolldown_j` from 4.1:

    hedged:    carry_earned_j = w_j (c_j/12 - r_short_local,j/12 + rolldown_j)
               funding_j = 0,  fx_j = 0
    unhedged:  carry_earned_j = w_j (c_j/12 + rolldown_j)
               funding_j = -w_j r_short_base/12,  fx_j = w_j fx_return_j
    both:      yield_change_pnl_j = w_j r_j - carry_earned_j - funding_j - fx_j
                                  = w_j (r_local_full,j - c_j/12 - rolldown_j)
    cost_t = -cost_t from the backtest

`r_j` is the variant's own return column as the backtest used it, which is
`0.0` for a **closed** bucket; a closed bucket contributes zero to every
piece, because it earned nothing.

The Taylor form is reported **beside** the identity and never inside it:

    yield_change_taylor_j = w_j (-D_j dy_j + C_j dy_j^2 / 2 - rolldown_j)
    taylor_residual_j     = yield_change_pnl_j - yield_change_taylor_j

with `dy_j` the step-4.2 `dy` (the change in the bond's own yield to
maturity, the #17 ruling) and `D_j`, `C_j` the 4.2 duration and convexity.

### Deviation 1 — the rolldown inside the Taylor bracket

PLAN.md 6.1 wrote the Taylor form as `sum_j w_j (-D_j dy_j + C_j dy_j^2 / 2)`
and in the same paragraph required `taylor_residual` to be "exactly the 4.2
`gap_bp` aggregated by the weights". Those two sentences cannot both hold.
The quantity being approximated is `r_local - c/12 - rolldown`; the bare
Taylor price term approximates `r_local - c/12`; the difference of the two
therefore carries a leftover `-sum_j w_j rolldown_j`, and
`test_taylor_residual_equals_weighted_gap` — the plan's own test — could not
pass. The rolldown is subtracted inside the Taylor bracket as well, which is
the only reading that satisfies the invariant the plan states. PLAN.md 6.1 is
amended to match in this session. The bare Taylor price term, for anyone who
wants it, is `yield_change_taylor + sum_j w_j rolldown_j`.

### Deviation 2 — decade rows in `decomposition_shares.csv`

Session-6 amendment 1 asks the review for the shares "cumulative and by
decade". The decade rows are written into the same file with the decade as
the `window` (`1990s`, `2000s`, `2010s`, `2020s`), so the schema is unchanged
and no second file is needed. A decade with fewer than 12 months of the
sample gets no row. PLAN.md 6.1 is amended to say so.

## Not verified

- The `share` column divides by the summed `r_net`, which is small and can be
  near zero over a short window; the 1990s shares (-13.9 and +14.2) are large
  for that reason and not because the pieces are large. The check that would
  settle it is the `cumulative` column, which is in the same file and is what
  the report quotes; the shares are only ever quoted beside it.
- The unhedged variants' `fx` piece has not been cross-checked against
  `data/checks/unhedged_fx_exposure.csv` (session-5 fix 2). Both are built
  from `returns.fx_return` and the same weights, so they should agree
  identically; the check would be a row-level join.
