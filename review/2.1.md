## What changed
- `src/curvecarry/bondmath.py` (new). `discount_factor(z, t) = (1+z)^(-t)` (annual compounding, the convention of every zero curve in the repo). `cashflow_times(tenor, freq)`: `tenor − k/freq` for `k = n−1 … 0`, `n = ceil(tenor·freq − 1e-9)`, ascending, all strictly positive, last entry exactly `tenor`; a seasoned bond's first entry is the short stub. `price_from_zero(curve, tenor, coupon, freq)` = `Σ (100·coupon/freq)·DF(t_j) + 100·DF(T)`, raising `ValueError` naming the cashflow dates above the longest observed tenor. `par_yield_from_zero(curve, tenor, freq)` = `freq·(1 − DF(T)) / Σ DF(t_j)`. `price_from_yield` (the ytm-compounded price, needed by the next two). `yield_from_price` by `scipy.optimize.brentq` on `[-0.05, 1.0]`, `xtol = 1e-12`. `modified_duration(ytm, tenor, coupon, freq)` = `Mac / (1 + y/f)`. `convexity(ytm, …)` = `Σ t_j (t_j + 1/f) CF_j v_j / (P (1 + y/f)²)`. `par_bond_risk(curve, tenor, freq) -> (c, D, C)` — **the one duration function**; 4.1, 4.2, 5.1, 5.2 and 6.1 call it and nothing else computes a duration.
- `_assert_zero` on the three functions that take a `Curve` (`price_from_zero`, `par_yield_from_zero`, `par_bond_risk`); `modified_duration` and `convexity` take a ytm, not a curve, so there is nothing to assert (rule 3 is about mixing curves).
- **Session-2 amendment 1** (`instructions/session-2.md`): `par_reprice(panel, zero, cfg)` and `write_par_reprice(cfg)` → `data/checks/par_reprice.csv`, 17,064 rows: for every US, JP and FR month and every **observed** par node (`interpolated == False` in `curves.parquet`), the observed par yield, `par_yield_from_zero(Curve.from_panel(curves_zero, …))` and the difference in bp. `Curve.from_panel` already kept every coupon-grid point of a bootstrapped month (Session 1 part B fix 1, issue #10 A), so no change to `curves.py` was needed; the check is what proves it.
- `src/curvecarry/checks.py` — five new paths: `PAR_REPRICE` (2.1), `FIT_SKIPPED`, `FIT_HOLDOUT` (2.2/2.3), `SVENSSON_VS_BUNDESBANK` (2.3), `CHART1_DATES` (2.4).
- `tests/test_bondmath.py` (11): the plan's eight, plus `test_cashflow_times_stub_and_grid`, `test_par_bond_risk_is_one_function` and `test_par_reprice_recovers_the_bootstrap` (a synthetic bootstrapped month, `|diff| < 1e-8` bp).

## Not verified
- **`convexity` has no finite-difference test.** The plan asks only for the closed form at coupon 0 and the duration finite difference. A second-difference check `(P(y+h) + P(y−h) − 2P)/(h²P)` would settle it; at `h = 1e-6` it is dominated by float noise, so it would need `h ≈ 1e-4` and a looser tolerance. Not added because the plan does not ask for it (rule 10).
- **`yield_from_price` outside the bracket.** A price implying a ytm below −5% or above 100% raises `brentq`'s own `ValueError` rather than a message of ours. No curve in the panel is anywhere near either bound.
- **`par_reprice` for DE, GB and CA.** The amendment names US, JP and FR — the three bootstrapped countries. DE, GB and CA publish zero curves directly, so there is no par node to reprice against.
