**Session 2 fix round (2026-09-23): the gap criterion is `ns_degenerate`, not `lam_at_bound`.** `ns_params.parquet` gains `ns_degenerate` = `lam_at_bound` **or** `max(|β0|, |β1|, |β2|) > 0.5` (50 percentage points of yield — a level factor of −22% is not a level factor), on every model; `lam_at_bound` is kept as its own column because `decisions/lambda_bound.md` refers to it. Chart 3's lower panel gaps on `ns_degenerate`, and `chart3_excluded.csv` gains a `reason` column: `bound`, `beta` or `both`. This closes the second finding below, which `lam_at_bound` alone had left open. **`ns_dl` trips neither criterion in any month of any country** — its largest max |β| is 0.3365 (GB) against the 0.5 threshold — so the fixed-λ panel has no degenerate month and is gapless by fact, not only by construction. Full counts in the #13 comment.

**Chart 3 is built as issue #14 (option K) directs**, not as PLAN.md v2 had it. Top row: β0, β1, β2 from **`ns_dl`**, fixed λ — the primary series, because with a free λ the betas are coefficients on loadings whose shape changes month to month, so the same numeric value in two months is not the same quantity. Bottom row: the same three from `ns_free` with every `lam_at_bound` month **left as a gap** — NaN in place, so the row stays on the x axis and the line breaks rather than being drawn across. Gap counts per country in the caption. **No percentile clipping anywhere**; my own earlier suggestion of it is withdrawn. Chart 1 and `chart1_rmse` keep NS-free, since what they measure is fit quality.

**Deviation 1, stated in full: Chart 1 draws the fitted models as curves, not as polylines through the 8 fitted tenors.** The plan says "observed points, NS-free line, Svensson line". Drawing the line through `ns_fitted.parquet`'s 8 points joins them with straight segments, which is a picture of linear interpolation, not of Nelson-Siegel or Svensson — and the shape the model puts *between* the observed points is the only thing Chart 1 can show that the RMSE table cannot. `chart1_fit` therefore evaluates each model from its parameter row on a 300-point grid from 1 to 30 years (`fitted_curve`). On US 1999-12-31 the two readings differ visibly: the polyline showed NS-free flat from 10y to 20y, the curve shows a hump peaking near 14y at 6.92%, and Svensson's second hump at 16y at 7.08% only exists in the curve version. `ns_fitted.parquet` is unchanged and still holds the 8 fitted tenors; nothing else reads the dense grid.

**Deviation 2.** Five tests rather than the plan's two — the third is the amendment's, the fourth covers deviation 1.

**Deviation 3, revised in the session 2 fix round (2026-09-23).** The plan says a country lacking a decade falls back to its earliest fitted month. It no longer does: **`chart1_dates` emits no row for a decade in which the country has no fitted month**, so `chart1_dates.csv` holds only real dates (23 rows, not 24 — FR 1990 is gone), and `chart1_fit` draws an **empty panel captioned "no fitted months in this decade"** for the gap. The fallback put France's 2004-11-30 curve under a panel headed by the 1990s; an empty panel says the true thing instead. Every figure still has one panel per configured decade, so the six countries stay comparable side by side. Two consequences: the legend now goes on the first panel that has data rather than on panel 0 (FR's panel 0 is the empty one), and a country with no fitted month in *any* configured decade gets no figure at all rather than a sheet of empty panels. What survives of the original rule: where a decade has months but no fitted December, its **last fitted month** is used — which is why the plan's own 24th row was never reachable for FR in the first place.

## Not verified
- **Nothing asserts that the dense Chart 1 curve agrees with `ns_fitted.parquet` at the 8 tenors.** They agree by construction — the same loadings and the same betas — and `test_chart1_draws_the_model_not_a_polyline` checks only that the curve is defined off-node and that the Svensson curve differs from the NS one. A test comparing the two at the fitted tenors would settle it.
- **The pngs are checked for existence and size, not for content.** That is what the plan asks for. Anything stronger means an image hash, which would break on a matplotlib or font upgrade.
- **Whether the NS-free panel earns its place at all.** With CA and US a quarter gapped and the largest kept |β| still 44.5% (GB 1976-06-30, outside the chart window), it may be more honest as a table than a chart. Left as the owner specified it.
