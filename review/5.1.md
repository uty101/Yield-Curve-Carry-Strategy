**The signal, stated in full.**
`signal = (carry + 12 × rolldown) / duration`. `carry` is per year and
`rolldown` is per horizon month (4.1), so the `12` puts both on a per-year
basis before the division; the units are return per year per year of
duration. Ranking is invariant to the scaling.

**Eligibility, stated in full** (session-5 amendment 3 adds the second
clause). A bucket is eligible at `(country, tenor, month)` when, in this
order:

1. `carry`, `rolldown`, `duration` are finite and `duration != 0` —
   otherwise `missing_input`;
2. the bucket is in that month's row of `data/checks/universe_by_month.csv`
   — otherwise `not_in_universe`;
3. `yield ≥ config.signal.yield_floor` (0.002) — otherwise `below_floor`.

One row carries one reason, the first that applies. An ineligible row keeps
its signal: the number is still the number, it is just not rankable.

## Not verified

- That `universe_by_month.csv` is itself complete. It is 4.2's output and
  was reviewed there; 5.1 takes it as given and only joins to it. The check
  that would settle it is the one 4.2 already ran (`return_coverage.csv`).
- That the 20 bp floor is the right floor. It is the brief's number and is
  read from config; `test_floor_read_from_config_not_hardcoded` shows only
  that changing it changes the answer, not that 20 bp is correct.

## Open

- The `not_in_universe` count is dominated by one month (2026-08-31, 48 of
  62). That is the sample edge and is correct, but it means the check file's
  column total is not a useful summary number on its own; read it per month,
  which is why the file is per month.
- JP churns in and out of eligibility at the short end far more than any
  other country (the 1-, 2- and 3-year buckets enter and leave 20+ times
  between 1999 and 2024). It is the floor doing exactly what the brief asks,
  but it will make JP turnover in 5.3 higher than the others, and that is
  worth naming before the cost number is seen rather than after.
