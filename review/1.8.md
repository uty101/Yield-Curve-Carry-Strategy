Date: 2026-09-22   Commit: (next commit)   Tests: 106/106

## What changed
- `src/curvecarry/interp.py` (new) — `interpolate_yield(tenors, yields, target)`: the one interpolation function (rule 13). Linear in tenor between adjacent observed tenors; exact at an observed tenor; NaN for any target outside `[min, max]`; NaN inputs dropped first; `ValueError` with fewer than 2 finite points or duplicate tenors. The flat short end is not here.
- `src/curvecarry/curves.py` (new) — `Curve` frozen dataclass per PLAN.md Conventions (`tenors, yields, curve_type, country, date`; sorted, NaNs dropped, arrays read-only), `Curve.from_panel` (every observed tenor of a country-month; rows with `interpolated == True` excluded when the column exists), `Curve.at(t)`: `interpolate_yield` inside the observed range, **`yields[0]` below the shortest observed tenor** (the one place the flat short end lives, `config.short_end`), NaN above the longest. **The plan assigns `curves.py` to no step**; it is built here because 1.8's `interp.py` text says `Curve.at` calls it and 1.9's bootstrap takes a `Curve`.
- `src/curvecarry/harmonise.py` (new) — `standard_tenors(cfg)`, `to_standard(curve_df, cfg)` (per country-month: every observed tenor kept; a standard tenor not observed is added by `interpolate_yield` only when it lies strictly between the shortest and longest observed tenor, flagged `interpolated = True`; `standard = tenor_years ∈ config.tenors`; mixed `curve_type` in one country-month refused), `cut_to_strategy_end(df, cfg)` (Session 2 amendment 1: months `> config.sample.strategy_end` dropped; `data/interim` untouched), `build(cfg)` → `data/processed/curves.parquet` + coverage `stage = harmonised` for the standard tenors.
- `src/curvecarry/cli.py` — `STEPS = {"harmonise": ...}`; `build --step harmonise`; `build --all` runs the loaders then the build steps.
- `tests/test_interp.py` (7: the plan's four `test_interp_*` plus `test_curve_at_flat_short_end_and_nan_long_end`, `test_curve_sorted_nans_dropped_frozen`, `test_curve_from_panel_takes_observed_rows_only`), `tests/test_harmonise.py` (9: the plan's `test_standard_tenors_from_jp_like_input`, `test_standard_tenors_from_boc_like_input`, `test_non_standard_tenors_kept`, `test_no_funding_row_on_curve`, `test_one_curve_type_per_country_month`, `test_no_forward_fill`, `test_panel_stops_at_strategy_end` (amendment), plus `test_interpolated_rows_are_flagged_and_inside_range_only`, `test_interp_import_is_the_only_interpolator`).
- `data/checks/coverage.csv` — 48 `harmonised` rows (6 × 8).

## Not verified

Checked month by month: 0 months in 1978–80 have 1y present and 2y absent, which is why no JP row is interpolated.
- `Curve.from_panel` on the processed panel relies on the `interpolated` column to exclude added rows; a caller passing an interim CurveFrame gets every row, which is also every observed row. Not exercised on the real panel yet (2.1 will).
