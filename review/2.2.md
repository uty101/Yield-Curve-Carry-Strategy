## What changed
- `src/curvecarry/nelson_siegel.py` (new). `ns_loadings(tau, lam)` — the brief's 6.1 form in λ (λ = 1/τ_decay), `[1, (1−e^{−λτ})/(λτ), (1−e^{−λτ})/(λτ) − e^{−λτ}]`, with the τ = 0 limit `[1, 1, 0]` handled explicitly. `fit_fixed_lambda(tau, y, lam)` — OLS by `np.linalg.lstsq`, RMSE returned in **decimal**. `lambda_grid(grid)` = `np.arange(lo, hi + step/2, step)`. `fit(tau, y, grid)` — the grid, then `minimize_scalar(method="bounded", xatol=1e-10)` on `[λ*−step, λ*+step] ∩ [lo, hi]`, keeping the polished λ only if its RMSE is no worse than the best grid point. `NSFit(beta0, beta1, beta2, lam, rmse_bp, n_tenors)`. `fit_panel` → `data/processed/ns_params.parquet` (6,306 rows) and `ns_fitted.parquet` (47,184 rows), `model ∈ {ns_free, ns_dl}`, `ns_dl` at `config.nelson_siegel.ns_lambda_fixed = 0.7`. `skipped` → `data/checks/fit_skipped.csv`.
- **Session-2 amendment 3**, built here and completed in 2.3: `holdout_points`, `holdout_errors`, `holdout_summary`, `write_holdout` → `data/checks/fit_holdout.csv`. Fits still see the 8 standard tenors only, so every held-out point is a tenor no fit was shown.
- `src/curvecarry/cli.py` — `build --step ns`.
- `tests/test_nelson_siegel.py` (7): the plan's five, plus `test_holdout_points_split_by_country` and `test_holdout_error_is_zero_for_a_perfect_model` for the amendment.

**Deviations, stated in full.**
1. **`fit` keeps the grid winner when the polish is worse.** `minimize_scalar` on a bounded bracket can return a point with a higher RMSE than the grid point it started from (the objective is not convex in λ). The plan does not say which to keep; keeping the better of the two is the only reading under which `test_grid_refinement_not_worse_than_grid` can hold, and that test is the plan's own.
2. **Two extra tests** (amendment 3), as above. No plan behaviour changed.
3. **`fit_skipped.csv` counts a month with no standard tenor at all**, not only months present in the standard panel. No such month exists in the data (every JP row below is 2, 3 or 4 tenors), so the file is the same either way; the reindex is there so a future country cannot skip silently.

## Not verified
- **Whether the pinned λ costs accuracy.** The grid bound binds in a third of months, but the held-out ratios above are still near 1, so the pinning may be harmless. What would settle it: refit with a wider grid and compare both in-sample and held-out RMSE on the same months. Not done here — the grid is `config.nelson_siegel.ns_lambda_grid` and rule 14 forbids changing a default outside the step that says so. Raised with amendment 2's finding in the session review.
- **`ns_fitted.parquet` is only at the 8 fitted tenors.** The held-out fitted values are computed from the parameters in `holdout_errors`, not read back from this file. They agree by construction (same loadings, same betas) but nothing asserts it on the real panel.
- **CA's 75 bp held-out p95.** Canada's held-out set is its own quarter-year coupon grid, 52,052 rows — by far the densest — so its p95 is over the most points and the least smoothing. Not investigated.

## Open
Nothing that stopped the step. The λ-at-the-bound share is a finding, not a failure: every fit converged, and the amendment-3 ratios show the fits hold up out of sample.

**Session 2 fix round (2026-09-23).** `ns_params.parquet` gains `ns_degenerate` = `lam_at_bound` **or** `max(|β0|, |β1|, |β2|) > 0.5`, on every model, alongside `lam_at_bound` (kept, because `decisions/lambda_bound.md` refers to it). `ns_free` degenerate months: CA 169 (34.6%), US 174 (22.4%), GB 150 (22.1%), JP 106 (17.7%), DE 24 (6.9%), FR 12 (4.6%) — the beta criterion adds 2 GB months the bound missed and agrees on 60 more. **`ns_dl` is 0 in every country**: its largest max |β| is 0.3365 (GB), so the fixed-λ model has no degenerate month. `test_ns_degenerate_is_bound_or_a_runaway_beta`.

**The stop condition fired and the session stopped.** The owner's instruction was to stop and post a decision issue rather than widen again if lambda still pins at 0.05 in more than 10% of months for any country. It does in four of six (CA 31.6%, GB 19.4%, US 18.6%, JP 13.4%), so **step 2.4 is not built** and **issue #14** is open. An offline refit of all 544 pinned months on `[0.01, 1.5, 0.01]` (nothing committed) shows 66% to 89% of them would still pin at 0.01 for a median gain of 0.27 to 1.00 bp: lambda -> 0 is a degeneracy of Nelson-Siegel on a flat curve, not a grid-width problem. At lambda = 0.05 the slope loading is 0.975/0.787/0.518 at 1/10/30 years — nearly the level loading — and the betas run away to cancel: JP 1981-06-30 has beta0 = 2.409, beta1 = -2.314, beta2 = -2.595. The bound is the only thing holding them. Full argument and four options in #14.
