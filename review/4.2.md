**Definitions stated in full.** At `t` the bucket buys the par bond at tenor
`n`: coupon `c = par_yield_from_zero(curve_t, n, freq)`, price 100. At `t+1`
its tenor is `n − 1/12` and its dirty price is
`price_from_zero(curve_{t+1}, n − 1/12, c, freq)`; `r_local = P/100 − 1`. No
coupon falls inside the month for `freq ≤ 2`, so nothing is reinvested.
`r_excess_local = r_local − r_short_local/12`, with `r_short_local` the
bucket's own country at date `t`. A bucket whose aged tenor is beyond the
curve at `t+1` is closed: `r_local = 0.0`, `closed = True`, one log row. The
last month of each country's panel (2026-08) has no `t+1` and so produces no
rows at all — that is why the panel ends 2026-07.

**One deviation from the plan, and it is the whole of the first finding.**
`r_local_approx` and `gap_bp` are the plan's formula exactly as written and
are biased by about −30 bp a month. Two candidate repairs are computed
beside them in extra columns (`*_ytm`, `*_curve`) and in
`return_approx_gap.csv`, and the choice between them is a `decision` issue
of this session. Nothing downstream is affected: `r_local` is the full
repricing and the approximation is diagnostic.

## Not verified

- **That the `curve` candidate's 20- and 30-year drift is the par/zero wedge
  and not something else.** Its rolldown term is the zero-yield rolldown of
  step 4.1 while its `Δy` is a par move, and the two differ most where the
  zero and par curves differ most, which is the long end. The check that
  would settle it is to recompute the `curve` candidate with a par-yield
  rolldown at coupon-grid tenors only; it was not done because the choice
  between the candidates is the owner's.
- **That the identity's long-end failures are trend and not a repricing
  error.** The argument is that `price_return_ann` is positive wherever the
  flag is positive and negative wherever it is negative, which is what a
  yield trend looks like. Recomputing over a sub-sample chosen to have zero
  net yield change would settle it.

## Open

- **The plan's `Δy` cannot be what the plan meant, and the plan does not say
  what it meant instead.** Posted as a `decision` issue with both candidates
  and the numbers above. Until it is answered, `r_local_approx` and `gap_bp`
  hold the plan's formula, the test that asserts the plan's tolerance on it
  is `xfail(strict=True)`, and step 4.2's "Done when" is met only in that
  sense. Nothing downstream reads these columns.
- **The 2 bp tolerance is unreachable under the plan's `(D, C)` choice**, by
  about 4 bp for a 50 bp monthly move, and that is a separate question from
  `Δy`. It is in the same decision issue. Using the aged bond's `(D, C)`
  would close it, but the plan names `par_bond_risk(curve_t, n, freq)` and
  this step did not change that.
