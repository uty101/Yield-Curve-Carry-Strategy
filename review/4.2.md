**Definitions stated in full.** At `t` the bucket buys the par bond at tenor
`n`: coupon `c = par_yield_from_zero(curve_t, n, freq)`, price 100. At `t+1`
its tenor is `n − 1/12` and its dirty price is
`price_from_zero(curve_{t+1}, n − 1/12, c, freq)`; `r_local = P/100 − 1`. No
coupon falls inside the month for `freq ≤ 2`, so nothing is reinvested.
`r_excess_local = r_local − r_short_local/12`, with `r_short_local` the
bucket's own country at date `t`. A bucket whose aged tenor is beyond the
curve at `t+1` is closed: `r_local = 0.0`, `closed = True`, one log row. The
last month of each country's panel (2026-08) has no `t+1` and so produces no
rows at all — that is why the panel ends 2026-07.

**Issue #17 is answered and the deviation is closed.** `Δy` is the change in
the bond's **own yield to maturity**, the tolerance is **5 bp for tenors ≤ 10
and 10 bp at 20 and 30**, and `(D, C)` stay at `t` on the un-aged bond. The
`*_ytm` and `*_curve` candidate columns are dropped, so `returns.parquet`
carries exactly the schema PLAN.md 4.2 names; the `xfail` is gone and the
suite is green at 224. The first three findings below are kept as the record
of **why** the ruling was needed — the numbers in them are the ones the
decision was taken on, and they describe the *rejected* formula. PLAN.md 4.2
and the head of 6.1 carry the ruling; the post-ruling distribution is the
`return_approx_gap.csv` in the repo and the fix-round finding at the end.
