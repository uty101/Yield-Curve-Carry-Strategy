**The #19 ruling is implemented and the authority conflict is closed.**
The expanding PC2 is fitted **per country**, on that country's own change
rows dated ≤ `t`, on that country's own tenor set from 3.1, with 3.1's sign
convention and `pca_min_months = 60`. The contradicting sentence in the
`pca.py` module docstring was corrected in its own commit (`c3ce589`),
which also states what each step now takes: **5.4 per-country expanding,
6.2 pooled**. `PLAN.md` 5.4 carries the ruling and the owner's two reasons.
Nothing in 5.4 reads `pca_loadings.parquet` or `pca_scores.parquet` — those
are full-sample fits and would break rule 7 if a trading step read them.

**One consequence of the plan's NaN rule, found by test and kept.** `z` is
NaN until 36 scores exist, and the plan says a NaN `z` clears both armed
flags "until the next crossing". That applies to the warm-up window too, so
a country whose **first finite `z` is already beyond the entry threshold
does not trade on it** — it waits for `z` to cross `z_exit` and re-arm.
This is the literal rule and it is the conservative side: an entry on the
first number a 36-month window ever produces would be an entry on a
threshold the series has never been tested against. It is asserted in
`test_warm_up_nans_leave_the_overlay_disarmed_until_z_crosses` and stated
in the module docstring, so it cannot be mistaken later for a bug.

## Not verified

- That `z_entry = 1.5` and `z_exit = 0` are good thresholds. They are the
  config values the plan fixed, no variant in this session moves them, and
  the overlay's result is plainly sensitive to them. A threshold sweep is
  not in Phase 5 and would need its own pre-registered rows in
  `specifications.csv`.
- That a 2s10s pair is the right expression of a PC2 signal. The signal is
  fitted on the whole tenor set (up to 30 years for DE) but traded at two
  points of it. The plan fixes the pair; nothing here tests the alternative.

## Open

- **The overlay loses money hedged and makes money unhedged**, which is the
  same pattern as the carry book and for the same reason: the unhedged
  series carries a currency return that has nothing to do with the slope
  view. The hedged number, −0.31% a year at Sharpe −0.169, is the one that
  answers the question the overlay was built to answer, and the answer is
  that this mean-reversion rule did not work on this sample.
- **October 2008 is the clearest illustration and it is worth the owner's
  eye.** All five countries then trading entered *flatteners* at once,
  because curves had steepened violently and `z` was between +1.6 and +4.5.
  Curves went on steepening for another year. A rule that enters when every
  country agrees is a rule with no diversification left in it at exactly the
  moment it has the most risk on; the state machine has no cap on how many
  countries may be in the same direction. That is the plan's design and I
  have not changed it, but the combined book's 2008 drawdown is mostly this.
- The drawdown trough for `overlay_hedged` is 2026-05-31, i.e. the book is
  near its lows at the end of the sample. An 18-year drawdown is a strategy
  that does not work, not a strategy in a bad patch.
