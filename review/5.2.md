**The rule, stated in full.** Each month the eligible buckets are ranked by
`signal` descending, ties by `(country, tenor_years)`. `n` eligible,
`k = n // 3`; the top `k` is the long leg and the bottom `k` the short leg.
Every long bucket carries `wd = +B/k` years of duration per unit capital and
every short bucket `wd = −B/k`, `B = config.weights.long_duration_budget`
(5.0); the capital weight is `weight = wd / duration`. Neutrality is
therefore structural — `Σ wd = 0` because the legs are equal in count and
opposite in per-bucket duration — and not a fitted hedge ratio.

**Two readings the plan left to the step, both decided here and stated so
the reviewer can overrule them:**

1. **The 9-bucket minimum is counted book-wide in both scopes.** No single
   country ever has more than 8 eligible tenors, so a per-country reading
   would flatten the country-scope book for the entire sample. It is
   therefore the only reading under which the `duration_neutral_scope =
   "country"` variant of amendment 2 exists at all.
2. **`n_countries_that_month` in `B_c = B / n_countries_that_month` counts
   the countries that *trade*** (those with `k_c = n_c // 3 ≥ 1`), not those
   with any eligible tenor. Under the other reading a country with 1 or 2
   eligible tenors would take a share of the budget and not spend it, and
   the book's long leg would come out below `B` — which is the quantity
   amendment 4 asserts equals `B` every month.

## Not verified

- That `k = n // 3` is the right leg size. It is the brief's rule and is not
  a config knob, so it cannot be varied without a plan change; no variant in
  amendment 2 tests it. The check that would settle whether the result rests
  on it is a leg-size variant, which is not in this session.
- That the tie-break never matters in practice. `test_ties_are_broken_by_country_then_tenor`
  shows it is deterministic; the real panel has no exact signal ties (the
  signal is a float ratio), so the rule is untested against real data by
  construction.

## Open

- The plan's `test_third_rule` example "7 eligible → 2 and 2" can no longer
  happen under the default: 7 is below the minimum of 9 and the month is
  flat. The test keeps the example on an overridden minimum of 6, so the
  arithmetic is still asserted, and adds `9 → 3 and 3` on the real default.
  This is amendment 3 changing the plan, not the step deviating from it, but
  it is the one place where the plan's own wording no longer describes the
  default behaviour.
- `k` fell to 5 in early 2021. With `B = 5.0` that is a full year of duration
  per bucket in each leg, so a single bucket's yield move moves the book five
  times as much as it does in 2007. Nothing is wrong with it — the budget is
  the budget — but the 2020-21 months will dominate the vol, and that should
  be read alongside 5.3's drawdown dates rather than after them.
