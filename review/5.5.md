## Not verified

- That `V[SR]` across eleven in-sample variants is the right dispersion for
  the deflated Sharpe. Bailey & López de Prado take `V[SR]` over the trials
  of a search; here the eleven trials are one headline plus ten deliberately
  different books, not eleven attempts at the same thing, so `V[SR]` is
  probably wider than a true search variance and the deflation is on the
  conservative side. The check that would settle it is a real parameter
  sweep with its own logged rows, which is not in this session.
- That eleven is the honest `N`. It is the row count of
  `specifications.csv`, which is what the plan defines it as, and it counts
  only runs that were logged in this repo. It does not count the thinking
  that went into the plan before any of it ran.

## Open

- **The country-scope book is the one variant that changes the answer**:
  0.19% against 0.59%, at a third more turnover. That is worth a sentence in
  the report rather than a footnote. Splitting the budget six ways forces the
  book to take the best and worst of each country rather than the best and
  worst of the world, and the cross-country dispersion is apparently where
  most of the carry was.
- **`carry_hedged_no_gb30` has the same `config_hash` as `carry_hedged`.**
  Two different books share a hash because `exclude_buckets` is a variant
  field rather than a config knob (the 5.3 decision, taken to avoid adding a
  knob `PLAN.md` never named). The rows are still distinguishable by `label`
  and `note`, but if the owner would rather the hash separated them, a
  `[variants] exclude_buckets` key in `config.toml` is the fix and it is one
  commit.
- **The overlay's skew and kurtosis are extreme** (−4.2 and 54.4 monthly),
  which is what a rule that puts every country in the same direction at once
  produces. The deflated Sharpe's normality correction is doing real work
  there and the DSR of 0.009 should be read as "this did not work", not as a
  precise probability.
