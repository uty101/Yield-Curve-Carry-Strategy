**Deviations, stated in full (issue #10 for the first two):**
1. **`test_reprice_par_bonds_at_100` prices off the full coupon grid** (`bootstrap_grid`), not off the curve `bootstrap_par_to_zero` returns. The plan's two sentences conflict: the returned curve (observed + standard tenors) re-interpolates zeros linearly between 10, 20 and 30 years, and on the plan's own synthetic curve that misprices the 20y bond by 0.11–0.12 and the 30y by 0.63–0.67; the grid reprices to 1e-10. The test also asserts the returned curve is a subset of the grid to 1e-15. Which curve the panel should carry is #10, question A.
2. **The US 0.25 tenor is absent from the zero curve** (off the semiannual grid; no formula in the plan). It remains an interpolation node for `par.at`. #10, question B.
3. **Standard tenors below the shortest observed par tenor are not returned** (JP 1978-06..1980-07, shortest observed 4y: no 1y/2y/3y zero rows). The plan's "plus the 8 standard tenors ≤ T_max" would emit them from the flat short-end convention; that is a convention for stubs, not a value the panel may claim, and 1.8 leaves the same tenors absent. 42 rows fewer than the literal reading; no effect on the sample window (JP's binding tenor is 10y, from 1986-07).
4. `data/checks/sample_window_by_country.csv` (per-country first month with every standard tenor ≤ 10y) is an extra file the review-issue amendment asked for as a table.

- Whether the MOF historical file before 1986 applied the same cubic-spline / on-the-run selection as the current outline: the outline is undated and the Q & A only dates the price providers.
