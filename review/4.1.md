# Review — Step 4.1 — Carry and rolldown

Date: 2026-09-23   Commit: 93f0277   Tests: 202/202

## What changed

- `src/curvecarry/carry.py` — new. `carry = y_n − r_short` (per year),
  `rolldown = (y_n − y(n − 1/12)) · D` (per horizon month, decimal),
  `expected_return_1m = carry/12 + rolldown`. `D` and the convexity come
  from `bondmath.par_bond_risk` and from nothing else. `r_short` is read
  from `funding.parquet` at `(country, date, kind = hedge.funding_rate)`
  and is never taken off a curve.
- `src/curvecarry/checks.py` — the seven Phase 4 check paths.
- `src/curvecarry/cli.py` — `build --step carry`.
- `tests/test_carry.py` — new, 6 tests.
- `decisions/short_anchor.md` — a dated correction to the per-country
  short-end table; see the third finding.
- Written: `data/processed/carry.parquet` (23,680 rows, 1962-01 .. 2026-08)
  and `data/checks/carry_missing.csv` (48 rows, one per country-tenor).

**Definitions stated in full.** The bucket universe is the *standard* zero
rows of `curves_zero.parquet`: a bucket exists at `(country, date, n)` when
that country-month has a standard zero at `n`. `y_n` is that row's yield,
which equals `Curve.at(n)` by construction. `y_roll = Curve.at(n − 1/12)`:
linear in tenor between observed tenors, flat below the shortest observed
tenor, NaN above the longest. A bucket with no funding row that month, or
whose par bond cannot be priced, is dropped and counted in
`carry_missing.csv` — never filled.

## Not verified

- **That `r_short` is the right financing rate for a bond position.** It is
  the policy rate by the ruling of session 1, and this step only reads it.
  The check that would settle the cost of that choice is the
  `interbank_3m` robustness run of 5.5, not anything here.
- **That the 12 JP months whose curve starts at 2, 3 or 4 years are a data
  artefact rather than a real absence of a short benchmark.** They are
  counted (`n_flat_short_end` = 12 at both 2y and 3y) and nothing here
  depends on them. Reading MOF's per-month benchmark list would settle it.

## Open

- **Why GB's missing sub-1-year point is scattered rather than an early-sample
  run.** 46 separate runs across 56 years, several of them in 2013–2025, is
  not the shape of a series that simply starts late. It was checked against
  the raw BoE month-end archive only to the extent of confirming the panel
  reproduces it; the cause is on the BoE's side (months where its spline is
  not fitted below a year). It affects 128 GB 1-year bucket-months and
  nothing else, and in every one of them the rolldown is 0 rather than wrong.
