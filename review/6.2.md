### Definitions stated in full

    (a) vol_target       scale_t = min(target_vol / vol_{t-12..t-1}, max_leverage)
                         vol = sd(r_net, ddof 1) * sqrt(12) over the 12 returns
                         DATED t-12 .. t-1; scale 1 until 12 exist;
                         weight and wd both multiplied, so sum wd stays 0
    (b) dd_stop          drawdown of prod_{s <= t-1}(1 + r_s) against its running
                         peak (floored at 1.0); below -dd_stop the book is flat
                         for t+1 .. t+dd_reentry_months, then re-enters at the
                         base's own positions
    (c) rates_vol_filter pooled PC1 score, expanding, scope pooled, component 1,
                         pca_min_months months of history; v_t = sd of the 12
                         scores ending at t; flat at t when
                         v_t > expanding quantile_{<=t}(v, rates_vol_pct)

A flat month is a month with no position rows. That is identical to zero
positions for every number the backtest computes, and it keeps the turnover of
closing last month's book, charged in that month's cost.

The 2022 attribution is the 6.1 per-bucket pieces cut by country and leg, with
`hedge = funding + fx` (identically zero on a hedged variant) and the month's
cost allocated pro rata to `|weight|`, so the `country = ALL` row is that
month's `r_net` exactly.

### Deviation 3 — the pooled PC1 score is the cross-country mean

`pca.fit_pooled` stacks every country's changes, so a pooled fit has one
loading vector and one score **per country-month**, not one score per month.
PLAN.md 6.2 asks for "pooled PC1 score by the expanding method of 5.4" without
saying how the countries are collapsed. The score at `t` is the **mean across
the countries with a change row at `t`** of that country's own score,
`(change_{c,t} - mean_c) @ loadings[:, 0]`. A level shock is common across
curves, so the cross-country mean is the level move the pooled fit measures;
letting one country stand for the pool would make this a US filter. PLAN.md
6.2 is amended to say so.

### Deviation 4 — `pca_min_months` counts months, not stacked rows

The same function could read `pca_min_months` against the height of the
stacked matrix, which with six countries reaches 60 after ten months. It
counts **months of history**, as 5.4 does. PLAN.md 6.2 is amended.

## Not verified

- Whether a rates-vol filter whose expanding window **starts at
  `strategy_start`** would fire, and whether it would help. That is a
  different control from the one `config.toml` pre-registers, so running it
  now would be a specification chosen after seeing that the registered one
  does nothing. It is not run and not logged. The check that would settle it
  is a `decision` issue and a new config key, in a later session if the owner
  wants one.
- The vol-target scalar itself is not written to its own check file; it is
  recoverable as `wd_control / wd_base` per month from the two positions
  files. A check would be a join of the two.

## Open

- `carry_hedged_ddstop` flattens **five** months, not the three
  `dd_reentry_months` names, because the drawdown was still below the stop on
  the next two observation months and each breach re-arms the window. That is
  the rule as written ("if below `-dd_stop` the book is flat for `t+1 …
  t+dd_reentry_months`") applied month by month, not a bug, but the plan text
  does not say what happens when breaches overlap. Left as built and reported.
