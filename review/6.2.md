### Definitions stated in full

    (a) vol_target       scale_t = min(target_vol / vol_{t-12..t-1}, max_leverage)
                         vol = sd(r_net, ddof 1) * sqrt(12) over the 12 returns
                         DATED t-12 .. t-1; scale 1 until 12 exist;
                         weight and wd both multiplied, so sum wd stays 0
    (b) dd_stop          drawdown of prod_{s <= t-1}(1 + r_s) against its running
                         peak (floored at 1.0); below -dd_stop the book is flat
                         for t+1 .. t+dd_reentry_months, then re-enters at the
                         base's own positions
    (c) rates_vol_filter pooled PC1 score, expanding, scope pooled, component 1,
                         pca_min_months months of history; v_t = sd of the 12
                         scores ending at t; flat at t when
                         v_t > expanding quantile_{<=t}(v, rates_vol_pct)
    (d) ..._rolling     exactly (c), except the percentile is taken over a
                         rolling rates_vol_window_months (120) window of v

A flat month is a month with no position rows. That is identical to zero
positions for every number the backtest computes, and it keeps the turnover of
closing last month's book, charged in that month's cost.

The 2022 attribution is the 6.1 per-bucket pieces cut by country and leg, with
`hedge = funding + fx` (identically zero on a hedged variant) and the month's
cost allocated pro rata to `|weight|`, so the `country = ALL` row is that
month's `r_net` exactly.

### Session-6 amendment 9 — the dead threshold and the live test, side by side

Fix round 1 showed the expanding filter is a null **about its threshold, not
about its idea**. Amendment 9 pre-registered the same idea on a live
threshold, before the run existed: `rates_vol_filter_rolling` is identical to
`rates_vol_filter` in score, `v_t`, arming rule and application, and differs
only in computing the `rates_vol_pct` percentile on a rolling
`risk.rates_vol_window_months = 120` window of `v` instead of expanding from
1967. The key went into `config.toml` in its own commit (`406a876`) under
global rule 14, before the variant existed.

**The expanding version is a dead threshold; the rolling one is live.** In the
strategy window the expanding filter arms 349 months and fires 0 (max ratio
0.679); the rolling filter arms the same 349 and fires **31**, which is 8.9%
of them and almost exactly the 10% the rule implies.

Both are reported. The expanding run is **not withdrawn**: it was
pre-registered, it is logged, and rule 6 does not let a logged run be taken
back because it did nothing.

### Deviation 3 — the pooled PC1 score is the cross-country mean

`pca.fit_pooled` stacks every country's changes, so a pooled fit has one
loading vector and one score **per country-month**, not one score per month.
PLAN.md 6.2 asks for "pooled PC1 score by the expanding method of 5.4" without
saying how the countries are collapsed. The score at `t` is the **mean across
the countries with a change row at `t`** of that country's own score,
`(change_{c,t} - mean_c) @ loadings[:, 0]`. A level shock is common across
curves, so the cross-country mean is the level move the pooled fit measures;
letting one country stand for the pool would make this a US filter. PLAN.md
6.2 is amended to say so.

### Session-6 fix 1 — the rates-vol null is diagnosed, not asserted

The first review reported the filter as a null. A null and a no-op look
identical from `months_flat = 0`, so the null is now shown rather than
stated. Four candidates were checked and ruled out, and the fifth is the
answer:

| candidate | ruled out by |
|---|---|
| the percentile includes the current month, so nothing can exceed it | `out["threshold"] = out["vol"].expanding().quantile(pct)` does include it, and that is PLAN.md 6.2's wording ("up to and including `t`") — but at `n` observations the linear-interpolated 90th percentile sits at position `0.9 (n - 1)` and a new maximum at `n - 1`, so a new high clears it. 67 triggers prove it empirically, the first at 1968-01-31 with vol 65.234 against threshold 65.122. Recomputing the percentile **excluding** the current month leaves the in-sample max ratio at 0.6788, unchanged to four places |
| the comparison uses the wrong series | `out["vol"] = s.rolling(window, min_periods=window).std(ddof=1)` over `s = out["score"]`, then `out["triggers"] = out["vol"] > out["threshold"]` where `threshold` is the expanding quantile **of `vol`**. Both sides are the vol series |
| the expanding window never reaches its minimum | 705 of 775 months are armed, and **all 349 sample months are armed**. `armed` is now a column |
| the flag is computed but never applied | `test_rates_vol_flag_is_applied_to_the_weights`: raising the flag on one month removes that month's position rows. Against the real book, mapping the flag over `positions_carry_hedged.parquet` produces **0 unmatched dates**, and an injected flag on 2022-02-28 drops all 20 of that month's rows |
| it genuinely never triggers on this data | **this one** |

The rule is sound and fires at **9.5% of armed months** over its whole life,
which is the ~10% an expanding 90th percentile implies. All 67 triggers are
pre-1983. The threshold is set by 1962-1982, when pooled PC1 vol ran two to
three times its post-1997 level, and a 90th percentile decays far more slowly
than the series did: by the 2020s the threshold is still 118 bp against a
trailing vol of 38 bp. Inside the sample the filter never gets past 0.679 of
the way to firing.

`data/checks/ratesvol_filter.csv` now carries `ratio`, `armed` and `triggers`
per month (renamed from `rates_vol_filter.csv`), and three tests keep a future
null distinguishable from a no-op:
`test_rates_vol_flag_is_applied_to_the_weights`,
`test_rates_vol_filter_fires_on_a_series_that_should_fire` and
`test_rates_vol_ratio_is_written_for_every_armed_month`.

No bug, so nothing was re-run: `carry_hedged_ratesvol` and
`combined_hedged_ratesvol` keep their original rows and **N stays 17**.

### Deviation 4 — `pca_min_months` counts months, not stacked rows

The same function could read `pca_min_months` against the height of the
stacked matrix, which with six countries reaches 60 after ten months. It
counts **months of history**, as 5.4 does. PLAN.md 6.2 is amended.

## Not verified

- Whether a rates-vol filter whose expanding window **starts at
  `strategy_start`** would fire, and whether it would help. That is a
  different control from the one `config.toml` pre-registers, so running it
  now would be a specification chosen after seeing that the registered one
  does nothing. It is not run and not logged. The check that would settle it
  is a `decision` issue and a new config key, in a later session if the owner
  wants one. What **is** now measured is how far the registered one is from
  firing: `ratio` in `data/checks/ratesvol_filter.csv`, max 0.679.
- The vol-target scalar itself is not written to its own check file; it is
  recoverable as `wd_control / wd_base` per month from the two positions
  files. A check would be a join of the two.

## Open

- `carry_hedged_ddstop` flattens **five** months, not the three
  `dd_reentry_months` names, because the drawdown was still below the stop on
  the next two observation months and each breach re-arms the window. That is
  the rule as written ("if below `-dd_stop` the book is flat for `t+1 …
  t+dd_reentry_months`") applied month by month, not a bug, but the plan text
  does not say what happens when breaches overlap. Left as built and reported.
