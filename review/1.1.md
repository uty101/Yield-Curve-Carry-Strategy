Recorded in `decisions/sources.md` (DGS30 entry) and PLAN.md 1.1. `curves_us.parquet` rebuilt: 6,726 rows (was 6,773; 47 fewer).

## Not verified
- FRED's daily files include weekend/holiday rows as `"."`; `parse` drops them by NaN, so a month whose last business day is a `"."` (a holiday) samples the previous print. Not exercised on a real case in the tests; visible in coverage as nothing (no missing month arises from it).
